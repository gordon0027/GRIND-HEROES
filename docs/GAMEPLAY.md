# Gameplay

Grind Heroes is an automatic pixel-art RPG: choose a party, collect GOLD and
chests, equip loot, upgrade heroes, then push the next stage. No manual skill
button or premium purchase is required for combat progression.

## Where to go

| Screen | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Play | Watch automatic combat, open Stage/Boss Chests, choose farming or auto advance. |
| Inventory | Select a hero, compare items, equip/unequip six slots or use Equip Best. |
| Team | Recruit heroes, buy party capacity, assign up to three owned heroes and select a pet. |
| Hero Progression | Open Upgrade Hero from Inventory or Team; preview GOLD costs, stat gains and skill milestones. |
| Market | Browse, list or buy eligible equipment for GEMS through iDos Marketplace. |
| Rankings | Server-derived Team Power and the protected Top 100. |
| Earnings | Read GH reward accounting and payout status; claiming is unavailable. |

## Classes

| Hero | Role | Signature at Hero Level 5 | Passive at 8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Knight | Frontline melee | Crushing Blow, 250% strike, 8-second cooldown | Guardian's Resolve, one low-health shield per run |
| Archer | Ranged damage | Rapid Fire, +200% attack speed for 3 seconds, 14-second cooldown | Piercing Precision, every fifth attack is empowered |
| Mage | Ranged area damage | Meteor, 180% area damage, 12-second cooldown | Scorching Impact, short refreshing burn |

Hero Level 10 masters the signature. Levels 1–4 still use normal attacks and
class movement/projectiles. Abilities are automatic after unlocking.

## Stages and farming

There are 30 finite stages in three acts: Meadow Road, Orange Bastion and Undead
Crypt. A validated clear earns GOLD, XP and a chest; stages divisible by five
also award a Boss Chest. The first clear grants Traveler Boots. Cleared stages
can be farmed automatically. Auto advance tries the next unlocked stage; defeat
returns to suitable cleared-stage farming and turns advance off.

Recommended Power is preparation advice, not an eligibility gate or promise.
Equipment, skill timing, frontline survival and party composition matter beyond
the score. An unprepared starter loses around 1-5; the game gives a recoverable
reason to equip loot and spend GOLD.

## Equipment and pets

Six slots: Helmet, Armor, Gloves, Boots, Weapon and Offhand. Items may be class
restricted. Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary need Stage Mastery 1/5/10/15/20;
these are not GOLD-paid Hero Levels. Equipped items increase effective combat
stats. Inventory comparisons and confirmed Power feedback use that same pipeline.

One owned pet can support the party. Selection changes apply at the next run,
so switching pets does not heal or rewrite an active fight. Donkey is available
for GOLD; other pet offers can use GEMS or GH. See [Economy](ECONOMY.md).
