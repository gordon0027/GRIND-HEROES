# Jury demo: Try Grind Heroes in 2 Minutes

Jury Edition is prepared in this branch. The public live game remains the existing
release until an authorized deployment; its UI and skill milestones may differ.

| Time | Action | What to explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Open the game and choose Play as guest. | Heroes fight automatically; early stages welcome a fresh player. |
| 0:15–0:40 | Open Team → Upgrade Hero, inspect Upgrades and Skills. | GOLD buys Hero Level. Signatures unlock at 5, passives at 8, mastery at 10. The starter 500 GOLD covers Level 2. |
| 0:40–1:15 | Return to Play and watch the first boss. | A measured fresh starter run clears 1-1 in about 68 seconds. Wait for server-confirmed rewards. |
| 1:15–1:35 | Open the Stage Chest; visit Inventory and Equip Best. | The first clear also gives guaranteed Traveler Boots. Gear changes actual combat stats. |
| 1:35–1:50 | Open the Stage Map, then Team/Pets. | Recommended Power is guidance, party slots and recruited heroes are separate investments, pets support the next run. |
| 1:50–2:15 | Browse Market, Rankings and Earnings. | Equipment trading uses GEMS; GH has Solana/platform Store utility. Earnings distinguish allocations from confirmed receipts; payouts are unavailable. |

Allow extra time for connection latency or exploration. There is no compulsory
long tutorial. The Next Step card prioritizes earned chests, available gear and
an affordable hero upgrade. At the first wall, farm a cleared stage and improve.

## Truthful longer-term goals

Level 5 needs 4,063 GOLD from Level 1 (~23 additional active farming minutes in
the measured scenario after starter GOLD). Recruiting a full party takes longer.
Do not promise these milestones in two minutes. The existing [technical demo](https://youtu.be/kQVqU1hUUec)
and [founder pitch](https://www.loom.com/share/d6565085ef3d405696bf17fff49a7032)
provide context; they were recorded before Jury Edition.

Use local DEV combat QA for class/milestone demonstrations when necessary. Such
previews are labelled, award no resources and never alter ownership or production
progress. Do not present a preview as an earned guest progression run.

## Verification notes

A fresh DEV browser guest cleared 1-1, earned a chest and boots, equipped gear,
upgraded through the native server and encountered a genuine early defeat/rollback.
An isolated SDK run verified exact level costs, ownership rejection, concurrent
absolute targets, insufficient funds, the cap, replay, reload and server Power.
See the [Russian release report](JURY_EDITION_REPORT_RU.md) for evidence and limits.
